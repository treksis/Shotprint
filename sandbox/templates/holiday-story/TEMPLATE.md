# holiday-story: the gift that brings back who you were

**The film.** A 120 s department-store Christmas film (brand removed; the store is `{BRAND}`). On a busy Christmas morning a teenage son sits shut away in over-ear headphones while his father kneels at the tree. The father finds a flat red parcel with a yellow sticky note reading "DAD". Inside is a vinyl record. He lowers the needle and a slow, intimate cover of a famous 1990s house song starts. Hard cut on the bar: he is dancing in a dark blue club among people half his age, in the same clothes. The crowd falls away and he dances alone in one warm spotlight. Then his son appears out of the black at every age: teenager, toddler, a laughing baby in his arms. Back home, eyes wet, he looks up. The son comes down the stairs with his headphones now round his neck, and they hug. The son grins, both laugh. Soft-focus living room, one thin white super, the wordmark.

**Arc.** Distance (headphones, a closed-off teen) -> the gift (a note, paper, a record, a needle) -> the memory (club, spotlight, the son growing up in reverse) -> together (stairs, hug, grin) -> end card.

**Rules that make it this film**
- Nobody explains anything. After 17 s there is no dialogue at all: the song carries everything. The only words are the hand-written note "DAD", the sleeve title and the end supers.
- The needle drop is the hinge. The click lands exactly when the stylus touches. The song enters soft, and the club version hits on a hard picture cut on the bar.
- The memory is a different world: a blue club grade at brightness 25-40, then a black void with one warm key (5-35), against warm amber home light (55-140). The father wears **the same clothes** in the memory.
- One colour links the ages: the son's teal-blue knit (teen sweater, baby romper).
- Close and shallow: 50-85 mm feel, inserts on hands and paper, low angles on the father with the record, top-down for the spotlight. The camera is steady or handheld-gentle, with slow arcs and pushes. No whips and no zooms.
- The headphones are the barometer: on his ears in the opening, round his neck at the reunion.
- End card: soft-focus home, thin white wide-tracked capitals at the centre: `FIND THE GIFT THAT SAYS IT`, then `{BRAND}`.

**Render plan.** Emotional close-ups (S04, S12, S14, S36, S41, S43) are built from first + last frame plates. Inserts (S08, S11, S13) also use first + last plates, with the product sheets as reference. The club flashback (S17, S24) is its own location and grade: build its plates from `loc_club_floor` / `loc_club_spotlight`, never from the home plates. The memory baby shot (S36) is on black. Draw the end-card text in post.
